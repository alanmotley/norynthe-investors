Capital Architecture Under Consideration

Capital type should match the capability being financed.

The $2M target is a laboratory funding objective, not a claim that every component should use one financing instrument. Norynthe is evaluating a staged, blended structure and has not selected or offered investment terms.

18-Month Proof Program

Capital deployment is staged against evidence, not calendar optimism.

The funded phase is designed to retire technical, methodological, institutional, and commercial risk in sequence. Dates remain planning ranges until facility, hardware, and external-review diligence is complete.

Months 0–3

Specify and govern

Finalize laboratory requirements, buy-versus-lease analysis, hardware configuration, security model, method register, benchmark register, and conflict controls.

Months 3–6

Establish controlled execution

Commission the minimum viable environment, evidence ledger, version controls, chain of custody, and first complete verified run package.

Months 6–12

Demonstrate repeatability

Publish repeated-run variance results, reconcile benchmark and method versions, complete external methodological challenge, and issue traceable pilot reports.

Months 12–18

Test institutional demand

Complete bounded design-partner engagements, test willingness to pay, document decision impact, and determine which recurring products evidence supports.

Commercial Hypotheses

One initial paid workflow; multiple later paths to validate.

The first commercial test is a scoped enterprise adoption-evidence engagement. The additional paths below remain hypotheses until buyer behavior and delivery economics support them.

Sole Initial Paid Model

Scoped adoption-evidence engagement

One consequential institutional decision, one bounded evaluation record, and one decision-ready report.

Later Unvalidated Hypothesis

Enterprise subscriptions

Later Unvalidated Hypothesis

Later Unvalidated Hypothesis

Advisory services

Later Unvalidated Hypothesis

API access

Later Unvalidated Hypothesis

Benchmark or sector licensing

Commercial Proof Sequence

Scale follows laboratory evidence and buyer validation.

Phase 1

Infrastructure foundation

Secure lab infrastructure, compute, legal structure, and the controlled operating base for independent evaluation.

Phase 2

Larger-model testing

Execute controlled evaluations across locally hosted and API-accessed systems, preserving complete run packages and measuring repeatability.

Phase 3

Enterprise engagement

Engage procurement, governance, risk, compliance, and enterprise AI stakeholders.

Phase 4

Controlled pilots

Run pilot programs and validate paid access use cases.

Phase 5

Validated packaging

Use observed demand and delivery economics to decide whether any recurring access, monitoring, API, licensing, or advisory extension is warranted.

Forecast Treatment

Commercial forecasts are withheld until the first offer is validated.

Prior internal scenarios are not presented as investor guidance. Revenue development depends on customer discovery, procurement stakeholder validation, pilot conversion, pricing acceptance, delivery capacity, and demonstrated willingness to pay. A board-usable forecast should follow evidence on cycle length, pricing, gross delivery effort, repeat usage, and conversion.
Investor Interpretation

Laboratory first. Revenue modeled only after validation.

The raise is not designed to immediately maximize headcount. It is designed to give Norynthe enough independence, controlled laboratory capacity, legal structure, and focused execution time to produce credible AI evaluation evidence before scaling.
