Capital Plan / Building The Lab

$2M to establish the independent AI evaluation laboratory.

Norynthe is raising $2M to establish a controlled environment in which AI systems can be observed, challenged, compared, documented, and reassessed outside the model owner's control. The round builds the physical, computational, methodological, evidentiary, security, and operating infrastructure required to turn a founder-built prototype and published method into repeatable independent evaluation capacity.

Building The Lab

The lab is the product's control environment.

The lab frame matters because Norynthe is not just building a page, dashboard, or wrapper. It is building a controlled environment where larger AI systems can be compared, scored, challenged, documented, and translated into decision-ready reports.

Compute Lab

Controlled evaluation capacity

Secure the GPU, memory, storage, and controlled runtime substrate needed for larger-model benchmark tests, evaluator workflows, calibration experiments, and future private evaluation paths.

Benchmark Lab

Owned test surfaces

Build and version the benchmark bank so model standing is measured against Norynthe-controlled evaluation assets rather than one-off prompts.

Scoring Lab

Evaluator and resolver logic

Tune scorer, critic, resolver, confidence, flagging, and evidence behavior against larger models so Norynthe.Score remains explainable and repeatable.

Report Lab

Decision-ready outputs

Turn raw scoring evidence into customer-facing report surfaces that procurement, governance, risk, and technical leaders can actually use.

Customer Lab

Use-case validation loop

Test the lab against real buyer questions, enterprise workflows, stakeholder feedback, and early pilot packaging.

Governance Lab

Method and audit discipline

Document the rubric, versioning, calibration, evidence expectations, and review states so the lab can mature into a trusted independent evaluation institution.

Strategic Compute Unlock

Hybrid compute is one controlled layer of the laboratory.

The power-of-compute thesis is simple: Norynthe is not trying to become a frontier model lab. The point is to own enough of the evaluation substrate to control sensitive and repeatable work while retaining API access and other external capacity where it improves coverage.

Target System

W775-V10-L01 AI Workstation

NVIDIA GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra

This is a candidate local-compute configuration, not a final procurement commitment. It would support controlled local assessment, repeated execution, private benchmark work, and reduced dependence on external APIs where those capabilities matter.

No hardware configuration creates independence by itself. The value comes when local compute is integrated with governed methods, benchmark custody, run manifests, preserved evidence, review, correction, and reporting.

Private benchmark development Rubric hardening environment Evaluator comparison and calibration Report generation without permanent API dependence Production-scale benchmark execution Credible independent market signal

Before purchase, Norynthe should reconcile the final hardware specification, power and thermal requirements, storage needs, security controls, and timing against the $146,500 fully owned compute infrastructure assumption.
Facility Decision Gate

The investor case is a purpose-bound laboratory base, not founder real estate.

Because the controlled-base assumption is the largest use of funds, it must pass documented diligence before capital is committed. The investor case is evidence control and operating continuity, not status, headcount, or a conventional headquarters.

Functional Necessity

Does control materially improve the work?

Evidence custody, reproducibility, security, power, thermal management, hardware continuity, and benchmark protection must be demonstrably stronger.

Technical Suitability

Can the site safely support the laboratory?

Power, cooling, connectivity, fire protection, physical security, insurance, access control, and expansion limits require professional verification.

Economic Case

Is ownership better than leasing?

Compare purchase, lease, colocation, and hybrid options using full buildout, financing, tax, maintenance, insurance, and opportunity-cost assumptions.

Governance

Is the asset clearly company-controlled?

Title, use restrictions, related-party risk, founder benefit, board approval, conflicts, and exit treatment must be explicit and independently reviewed.

Capital Efficiency

Can the same proof be produced with less fixed capital?

If a staged or hybrid alternative can produce equivalent evidence sooner and with less risk, capital should follow that evidence.

12-18 Month Plan

The round should create measurable operating proof.

The capital plan should be sequenced around proof, not spending for its own sake. Each phase should leave behind a stronger lab, clearer evaluation evidence, and better institutional proof.

0-90 Days

Lock the lab foundation

Finalize facility and buy-versus-lease diligence, minimum viable environment, hybrid compute specification, legal/entity setup, evidence custody, security controls, method register, and benchmark register.

3-6 Months

Commission controlled execution

Complete the first verified run package across selected local and API-accessed systems, preserve manifests and outputs, and document exceptions, limitations, and review.

6-12 Months

Demonstrate repeatability and challenge

Measure repeated-run variance, reconcile method and benchmark versions, complete external methodological challenge, and issue traceable pilot-ready reports.

12-18 Months

Validate buyer signal

Complete bounded design-partner engagements, test willingness to pay, document decision impact, and determine which recurring products the evidence supports.

Risk And Mitigation

Make the plan credible by naming the hard parts.

A disciplined investor story should show that Norynthe understands the risks and has a lean plan to reduce them through staged proof.

Risk

Compute spend before market signal

Mitigation: Stage infrastructure purchases around final specifications, lab requirements, security needs, and customer-validation milestones rather than treating the workstation as an isolated trophy purchase.

Risk

Category education takes time

Mitigation: Use the investor portal, white paper, report surfaces, and pilot materials to translate independent AI trust scoring into buyer-specific workflows.

Risk

Method credibility must be earned

Mitigation: Version the benchmark library, document scoring logic, preserve evidence, and make calibration decisions inspectable.

Risk

Founder concentration

Mitigation: Keep the first phase founder-led while using disciplined documentation, AI-assisted workflows, and repeatable operating systems to reduce single-person dependency over time.

Why $2M

Enough to build the lab without overbuilding the company.

The round is sized to create real company progress without forcing premature scale. It gives Norynthe enough capital to secure the operating base, own the compute path, tune the existing evaluation system against larger models, and test the market with serious early users while staying intentionally lean.

The next question for investors should become sharper: not whether Norynthe can describe the need for independent AI trust infrastructure, but whether the company can turn that need into repeatable scoring, reports, pilots, and buyer demand.
